# Dev log 2026-10-22

## Late evening (21st): the wake word failed while face detection ran (REQ-008)

The maintainer tested the fixed detection prototype: the box was accurate and followed them, but "起来干活" hardly
woke the robot any more.

- Measured on the device with a rate sweep (0, 1, 2, 4 detections per second, 10 s each) and two new counters: CPU
  time of `audio_input` (the wake word task) and microphone DMA buffers dropped by the I2S driver
  (`on_recv_q_ovf`).
- The custom wake word (MultiNet7) alone uses 74-78 % of core 0. Detection runs on core 1 but shares PSRAM and the
  cache with it: during a detection the wake word nearly stops, the six DMA buffers (60 ms) overflow, and 8 % (1/s),
  20 % (2/s) or 30 % (4/s, the rate the maintainer had tested) of the audio was lost. The recognition probability
  had dropped to 0.20, exactly the threshold (0.70 without detection that morning).
- Fix: `CONFIG_SC_MIC_BUFFER_MS` (fork 1000), a pump task on core 1 at priority 9 that drains the DMA buffers into a
  PSRAM stream buffer the codec's `Read()` takes from, and back-pressure in the detection task (start only when less
  than 80 ms of audio wait). Sweep again: 0 lost at every rate, backlog peaks 170-270 ms. The maintainer said the wake
  word right after: recognised with 0.39.
- The first build did not pick up the new Kconfig symbol: changes to `Kconfig.projbuild` and `sdkconfig.defaults.sc`
  need `idf.py reconfigure` here.
- Suit transitions left the head lower each time (keys went back to the fixed awake pitch, 20 degrees): they are now
  offsets from the pitch the head had, and end there. Committed to `feat/claude-suit` as well.
- `dependencies.lock`: the component manager moved uart-uhci, dl_fft, esp_codec_dev, esp_sccb_intf, i2c_bus and
  iot_usbh_cdc to newer versions within their ranges when `human_face_detect` was added. All tests of the night ran
  on these; the maintainer chose to keep them.

## Night: face tracking behaviour (maintainer asleep, working alone)

Decisions taken alone, for review:

- **Where**: the idle director owns tracking (`idle_director_face.cpp`), so it never fights the glances, behaviours,
  suit keys or sleep for the head and the eyes. The detection task only publishes the latest `Sighting` and runs
  while the director asks for it (every update while awake with `face_track` or `face_box` on).
- **Eyes** follow at once (relative offsets, eased); **head** turns 70 % of the angle when the face is more than 5
  degrees off, only on frames taken 250 ms after the servos stopped, then asks for the next detection at once.
  Greeting with the Joy face after 2 minutes without a face or an interaction.
- **Settings**: `face_track`, `face_head`, `face_box` (debug overlay, off), `face_probe` (default 1/s) and the
  requested sleep switch `sleep` ("Sleeping": off = never sleeps; a sleeping robot wakes when it is switched off).
- **Stale frames**: the DVP driver has one buffer, so after a pause the frame in it is up to a second old; it is now
  dropped when the last grab is older than 100 ms.
- **Dark room**: the room was dark all night, so no face could be tested. The first frame was flat, the luma lane
  check picked the wrong lane; it now waits for a frame that is not flat and defaults to the measured even lane.
  Flat frames (a uniform black level, mean luma 28, not 0) and frames darker than mean luma 40 skip the detector.
  The robot fell asleep in the dark as designed, which stops detection.
- **USB link v1.3** so the robot can be checked and driven without touching it: `set` (settings panel keys; ack
  carries `val`) and `head` (degrees). sc-agent `POST /setting`, `POST /head`, `robot_settings` in `/status`; 148
  tests pass. Read back over USB: `sleep` 1, `face_track` 1, `face_head` 1, `face_box` 0, `face_probe` 1; an unknown
  key was rejected.
- **Yaw direction**: the upstream comment on `Motion::lookAtPoint` says positive yaw is left, the voice tool says
  "-128 as your left". With the room dark the camera could not tell, so `head` arms an IMU check: the gyro's
  component along gravity (positive = counter-clockwise seen from above, independent of how the sensor sits).
  `yaw +20` gave -45.8 deg/s, the way back +42.6 deg/s: **positive yaw is the robot's right**. The first tracking
  build had it the other way round; fixed before any face saw it, and a guard now stops head tracking after two turns
  in a row that leave the face further off.
- Not done: the behaviour with a real face (needs light and a person), tracking during conversations, making the
  feature a fork default (partition space, needs the maintainer).
- Test data from the prototype is gone: 344 frame dump lines removed from the serial log, two captured frames deleted.
